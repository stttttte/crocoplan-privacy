CrocoPlan Privacy Policy

Updated: November 1, 2026

Operator: Sidi Liu Contact: lllsssddd@icloud.com

This policy covers CrocoPlan for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Your project content

Projects, versions, plans, tasks, ideas, deliverables, files, relationships and revision history are stored on your device. The China mainland App Store region uses local storage without iCloud sync; use exported copies and Import to transfer work between devices. Other identified App Store regions may use Apple's private CloudKit database to sync this content. If the storefront cannot be identified, the app uses local storage with cloud sync disabled. Storefront changes take effect after quitting and reopening the app. Sync requires your Apple Account and depends on your connection, system scheduling and available iCloud storage. The developer does not operate a server for storing your project content and cannot view your private CloudKit database through the developer console.

On Mac, you may choose a data folder. The app accesses that folder within the authorization you provide. Preferences such as language, character motion and canvas layout are stored on the current device.

AI handoffs and external services

The current version has no built-in AI chat or third-party model API. Handoff text and batch prompts are assembled from your records on your device. Content leaves the app only when you choose to copy, share or export it. CrocoPlan does not automatically read external AI accounts, other chats or development projects, or upload attachments to an AI.

If you send content to an external AI, that provider's privacy policy applies. Imported reports can be previewed, and AI estimates never replace task acceptance automatically.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

This version has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell project content. Apple may process iCloud service and diagnostic information according to your system settings and Apple's policies.

Files, exports and support

The app reads files that you choose to import. Imported deliverable files become managed copies. Exported JSON copies may contain project content and attachments; you control their storage and recipients. Links that you open are handled by the system or browser and the destination's privacy policy applies.

If you email support, the developer uses the email address, explanation and information you choose to send to respond to your request, keeping it only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. Remove unnecessary private content from screenshots or logs before sharing them.

Retention, archiving and deletion

Archiving retains records and does not delete them. This version offers archive, restore and export, but does not have an in-app command to permanently erase the entire workspace. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove iCloud copies or a separately selected Mac data folder.

You can manage the app's cloud data using Apple's iCloud storage controls; available controls vary by system version. You manage local exports and external Mac data folders. Back up important content before using system deletion controls. The developer cannot sign in to or erase your private iCloud database on your behalf.

Children and changes

CrocoPlan is a general-purpose personal project tool with no children's social, advertising or public-content features. Animal artwork does not designate it as a Kids Category app. This policy will be updated if a future version changes how data is handled.

For privacy questions, contact lllsssddd@icloud.com.
